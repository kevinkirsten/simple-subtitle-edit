Simple Subtitle Edit - first time on macOS
==========================================

1. Drag "Simple Subtitle Edit" onto "Applications".
2. The first time: right-click the app in Applications › Open › Open.
   (The app is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks once.)
   On recent macOS, if there is no Open button: System Settings › Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, "Open Anyway".

Everything it needs (video player, ffmpeg, mkvmerge) is inside the app.

---

Primeira vez no macOS
1. Arraste "Simple Subtitle Edit" para "Applications".
2. Na primeira vez: botão direito no app em Applications › Open › Open.
   (O app não tem assinatura paga da Apple, então o macOS pergunta uma vez.)
   Se não aparecer o botão Open: System Settings › Privacy & Security, role até o fim,
   "Open Anyway".
